Privacy Policy for Block Nova

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Nova ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you play the App. Block Nova is a puzzle game published by NovaForge. We built the App to work without requiring you to create an account or provide any personal information to play.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • No account required — you can play the full game without signing in or providing your name, email, or any personal details.
  • Local game data only — your best score, selected theme, and in-game preferences are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Advertising & Third-Party Services

Block Nova displays ads through Google AdMob to support free play. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partner networks may automatically collect certain technical information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device type, operating system version, and general technical information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and completions)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies. We do not control what Google or its ad partners do with this data beyond what's disclosed in their own policies.

Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your device's advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

Children's Privacy

Block Nova is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with information, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that concerns you, please contact us using the details below.

Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, there is no personal data for us to secure. Any technical data processed by advertising partners is handled under their own security practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
